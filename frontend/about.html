About PCEA Membley School

A Christ-centered learning community committed to academic excellence, values formation, and holistic child development.

Mission, Vision, and Core Values

Our foundation is faith, discipline, and a commitment to preparing learners for life and service.

Mission

To evangelize through impactful education and practical skills grounded in Christian values.

Vision

To raise competent and compassionate learners who become principled leaders in society.

Core Values

Integrity, excellence, discipline, respect, servant leadership, and stewardship.

Word from the Headteacher

At PCEA Membley School, our mission extends beyond academic instruction. We are dedicated to shaping well-rounded individuals grounded in Christian faith, strong moral values, and a passion for excellence. As a Christ-centered institution, we intentionally nurture each learner’s spiritual, intellectual, emotional, and social growth within a supportive and disciplined environment. Through daily prayer, principled leadership, and a committed team of professional educators, we cultivate a culture of integrity, responsibility, and compassion. Our partnership with parents and guardians plays a vital role in this journey, ensuring that every child receives consistent guidance both at school and at home. We believe education is a calling and a ministry. Therefore, we strive to inspire our learners to develop confidence, critical thinking skills, and a heart for service. Our goal is to raise disciplined, resilient, and God-fearing young people who are prepared not only for academic success but also for meaningful contribution to society. At PCEA Membley School, we are committed to excellence in character, faith, and achievement—equipping every learner to shine as a responsible leader of tomorrow.

Rev Simon Githiora Njuguna

School Chaplain

The School Chaplain, a minister from Presbyterian Church of East Africa (PCEA) Membley Church, provides spiritual leadership and oversees all pastoral programmes for the learners. The chaplain supports the holistic growth of children by nurturing their spiritual, moral, and emotional well-being through Christian teaching, prayer, mentorship, and guidance.

Word from the Deputy Headteacher: MR OUMA

As Deputy Headteacher, I am entrusted with the responsibility of overseeing curriculum coordination, strengthening instructional practices, and ensuring the consistent implementation of high teaching standards across the school. At PCEA Membley School, we are committed to delivering a well-structured, competency-driven curriculum that nurtures academic excellence while developing critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills. Through continuous instructional coaching and professional collaboration with our teaching staff, we strive to maintain dynamic, engaging, and learner-centered classrooms. We believe that effective education requires both structure and inspiration. By closely monitoring academic progress, supporting teachers in adopting innovative methodologies, and aligning instruction with national education standards, we ensure that every learner receives quality education tailored to their unique potential. Our goal is not only to achieve outstanding academic results but also to cultivate disciplined, confident, and lifelong learners prepared to thrive in an ever-evolving world. Together with our dedicated staff and supportive parents, we remain steadfast in our mission to uphold excellence in teaching, learning, and character formation.

Leadership and Governance

PCEA Membley School is governed through a structured board and school leadership framework focused on accountability and excellence.

  • Policy oversight for curriculum quality, safeguarding, and institutional ethics.
  • Transparent stewardship of resources and operational planning.
  • Regular communication with parents through formal meetings and progress reporting.
